# AI for Everyone
### With the Technology We Have Today

**An open invitation to form the first international team building a more accessible, affordable, sustainable AI ecosystem.**

> What if we could connect today's AI models, existing computing resources, and global talent to deliver useful AI services to more people—without waiting years for another wave of massive infrastructure investment?

AI has advanced remarkably. Yet access, cost, privacy, resilience, and the financial sustainability of infrastructure remain serious challenges. We believe an alternative deserves rigorous exploration: a **distributed, community-powered AI ecosystem**, built collaboratively and tested against real-world evidence.

We are **not opposed to existing AI companies**. Their achievements make this exploration possible. We invite collaboration, interoperability, and reuse of existing technologies.

## Our founding principles

1. **Universal access:** Make useful AI affordable worldwide; explore free or subsidized public-interest access.
2. **Financial sustainability:** Validate full service costs, not just hardware costs, before making pricing promises.
3. **Privacy and security:** Minimize data collection; design safeguards into execution and governance.
4. **Fair compensation:** Explore transparent rewards for verified, useful contributions, without guaranteeing earnings.
5. **Open-source development:** Favor auditable, interoperable, reusable technology and transparent processes.
6. **Global, participatory governance:** Let founding members consult on institutional structure and safeguards.
7. **AI-agent-driven operations:** Investigate how auditable agents can coordinate network operations, support, and accounting under human oversight.

## We are starting with a team, not a corporation

Our first goal is to assemble **5–10 committed founding contributors** to investigate feasibility, establish shared principles, and design a small proof of concept. **No final legal entity, jurisdiction, ownership model, or technical architecture has been chosen.**

The initiative was originated by its founder, whose intended long-term position is **Founder and Permanent Strategic Advisor**, subject to formalization in the future charter. This advisory role is not unilateral control of community decisions or finances.

## Who should join?

AI/ML engineers, open-source maintainers, distributed systems specialists, security and privacy engineers, agent-orchestration developers, infrastructure operators, economists, governance experts, researchers, and motivated contributors.

## First 90 days (proposed, subject to community agreement)

- **Days 1–15:** Form the initial team, review the vision, and agree on collaboration rules.
- **Days 16–30:** Compare technical approaches and model full unit economics and risks.
- **Days 31–60:** Prototype a limited, safe workload-routing system and basic agent-assisted operations.
- **Days 61–90:** Measure costs, performance, privacy, and reliability; publish findings and decide whether to proceed.

These are proposed milestones, not commitments or proof of feasibility.

## How to participate

1. Read [VISION.md](VISION.md), [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), and [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
2. Open a GitHub Discussion in **Introductions** with your expertise, interests, and available time.
3. Join a discussion about feasibility, security, economics, governance, or AI agents.
4. Propose a focused research task or open an Issue; do not submit large implementations before architecture review.

**Participation is voluntary at this stage. No employment, compensation, equity, token allocation, or future financial return is promised.**

## Funding philosophy

We envision blended support from **grants, earned service revenue, and appropriate strategic investment**, subject to a future mission-protecting institutional structure. We are not currently offering an investment product or soliciting funds through this repository.
