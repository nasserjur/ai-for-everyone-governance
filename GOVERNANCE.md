# Provisional Governance — Consultation Draft

**Status:** Temporary collaboration guidelines only. Not a legal charter.

## Founding process

We aim to assemble 5–10 initial contributors with complementary expertise. Participation in early discussions does not automatically confer a legal title, fiduciary role, equity interest, or permanent decision-making right.

## Decision process

- Seek documented consensus for routine repository and research decisions.
- Record significant proposals, alternatives, and dissent in GitHub Discussions.
- Do not assume silence means consent.
- Defer legal formation, ownership, investment, compensation, and binding governance rules until the founding group agrees on a formal process and obtains relevant advice.
- Maintainers may moderate content and protect repository security; this is operational stewardship, not permanent institutional authority.

## Founder

The originator is recognized as **Founder** and is intended to hold a **permanent strategic advisory role**, to be defined in the future charter and subject to applicable law. Advisory continuity does not imply unilateral control over votes, funds, technical direction, or members' rights.

## AI-agent oversight

AI agents may propose and execute bounded low-risk tasks once approved. Material financial actions, high-impact security interventions, governance changes, and legal commitments require authorized human review. Agent actions should be logged, reviewable, and reversible where feasible.

## Conflicts and safety

Disclose material interests in vendors, investments, and proposed technology. Never share credentials or private datasets in public issues. Maintainers may remove abusive or unsafe content, documenting significant moderation decisions where appropriate.

## Future governance questions

The founding group will consult on institutional identity, representation, voting, charter amendments, appeals, financial transparency, licensing, and independent audits.
