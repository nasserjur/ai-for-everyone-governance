# Proposed First 90 Days

This is an invitation to collaborate, not a fixed delivery contract. Dates begin when a functioning founding group is assembled.

## Phase 1 — Team and shared questions (Days 1–15)

- Recruit 5–10 active participants with complementary capabilities.
- Confirm a lightweight collaboration process and issue owners.
- Agree on evaluation criteria and target user scenarios.
- Discuss licensing, code of conduct, and contribution rights before accepting code.

**Exit:** Named volunteer owners, published questions, and agreed pilot success criteria.

## Phase 2 — Feasibility (Days 16–30)

- Compare centralized, federated, and peer-to-peer deployment options.
- Identify models, hardware classes, and workloads for benchmarking.
- Build an end-to-end cost model including compensation, networking, security, and administration.
- Threat-model private data and untrusted nodes.

**Exit:** Public architecture options, risk register, and measurable pilot design.

## Phase 3 — Limited proof of concept (Days 31–60)

- Implement only after licenses and security review are agreed.
- Route non-sensitive test workloads among a small number of consented nodes.
- Record throughput, latency, availability, energy estimates, and total costs.
- Test one or two supervised agents for monitoring or support; no autonomous financial transfers.

**Exit:** Reproducible prototype results and security findings.

## Phase 4 — Review and next decision (Days 61–90)

- Publish benchmark and cost comparisons with limitations.
- Assess privacy, reliability, compensation feasibility, and governance readiness.
- Decide whether to expand, revise the model, or stop.
- Explore grant and partnership opportunities only after a credible plan emerges.

**Exit:** Transparent go/no-go recommendation and next-stage proposal.
