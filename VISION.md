# Founding Vision — AI for Everyone

## The challenge

AI offers extraordinary benefits, but its development and delivery are increasingly capital-intensive. Financial viability, concentration risk, unequal access, privacy, and infrastructure dependency deserve serious scrutiny. Not every investment is wasteful or destined to fail; the question is whether complementary delivery models can reduce costs and broaden participation.

## The opportunity today

Much of the technology needed to deliver valuable AI services already exists: efficient and open-weight models, developer tools, computing resources, and skilled contributors. We should not assume that affordable access must wait for future frontier breakthroughs. We must nevertheless demonstrate performance and full costs empirically.

## Seven commitments

1. **Access:** Pursue low-cost AI services and public-interest inclusion.
2. **Sustainability:** Measure end-to-end economics and build a resilient model.
3. **Privacy:** Protect users through secure, privacy-conscious architecture.
4. **Compensation:** Reward useful, verified contributions fairly where financially feasible.
5. **Openness:** Favor open-source, interoperability, transparency, and independent review.
6. **Participation:** Develop international, accountable governance collaboratively.
7. **Agent-managed operations:** Use AI agents to reduce administrative burdens with auditable permissions and human accountability.

## What remains open

The legal entity, jurisdiction, governance structure, technical stack, pricing, compensation mechanisms, and commercial arrangements require founding-member consultation and evidence-based decisions.

## Leadership and funding

The initiative's originator is the Founder and intended Permanent Strategic Advisor. The eventual charter will define this advisory position with accountability and conflict-of-interest safeguards, without unilateral governing authority.

Funding may combine grants, service revenue, and strategic investment under mission-protecting arrangements. No returns or financial benefits are guaranteed.

## Collaboration, not confrontation

Established AI firms, open-source communities, academic institutions, governments, infrastructure providers, and independent contributors are welcome to collaborate. This project seeks to complement, not antagonize, the AI ecosystem.
